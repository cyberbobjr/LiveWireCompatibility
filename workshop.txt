description=[h1]LiveWire Compatibility[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.22 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer · Requires LiveWire[/b]
description=
description=Many mods add electric machines that the game does not count: they only check that their square has power. With [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3821807453]LiveWire[/url], they run for free. This mod declares them to LiveWire, so that:
description=[list]
description=[*]the inverter bills what they draw while they run, like a fridge or a stove;
description=[*]they appear in the management software, in the Consumers tab;
description=[*]right-click a machine and choose Turn off or Turn on: a machine that is off draws nothing;
description=[*]a machine that is off, without power or cut by a breaker refuses to work: its options are greyed with the reason, and a use in progress stops.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Enable it once.[/b] It detects the supported mods you use and ignores the others: nothing to configure.
description=

description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Draws are estimates, based on real machines of the same kind.
description=[*]With LiveWire 2.2.0 or later, a machine switched on or off shows at once on the inverter screens.
description=[*]Already handled by LiveWire, nothing to do: [B42] Working Air Conditioner Units, Badlands Power, Industrial Works.
description=[*]Nothing to bill: Video Game Consoles, Vectrex, Palm Pilots and other items carried in the inventory or running on batteries.
